FAQs

Can time tracking help determine which cleaning jobs are profitable?

Yes, TimeBee’s time tracking software for cleaning services records cleaner hours against the relevant project or task, giving each job a labor cost based on the time worked and employee rates. That cost can then be viewed alongside client pricing in profitability reports, making it easier to see how much margin a contract is generating. Because both sides of the calculation come from the same underlying work records, managers can assess job profitability without piecing together separate operational and financial data.

Can I check whether cleaners clock in from the correct job site?

TimeBee gives managers location evidence alongside clock-ins, making it easier to check whether work started from the expected client site. Mobile clock-ins provide location context directly from the cleaner’s device, while a shared kiosk captures the cleaner’s face together with the time and location on recurring sites.

How do I handle time tracking when a cleaner works at multiple locations in one day?

For recurring client sites, a shared kiosk can give cleaners one place to clock in while capturing the time, location, and face associated with each entry. Cleaners who move between properties can instead log time from mobile against projects set up for the relevant client jobs, with location context attached to their clock-ins. Moreover, if one session ends up covering multiple assignments, the ime tracking software for cleaning companies can split the recorded time so each job keeps the hours that belong to it.

Can cleaning staff track time when they have poor or no internet access?

Yes, TimeBee continues recording time when offline and syncs the recorded data back to the dashboard once the connection returns. This keeps work records intact in basements, large facilities, and other areas with inconsistent network coverage, so cleaners do not have to reconstruct their hours later.

How can cleaning time tracking help with overtime that occurs unexpectedly?

TimeBee makes extra hours easier to spot by comparing recorded work against configured shift expectations. Once those hours are visible, managers can review them before payroll and apply an adjustment amount to the total pay. This gives cleaning businesses a clearer way to account for unplanned overtime.

Can staff correct missed or inaccurate clock-ins without creating payroll problems?

Yes, reliable software for janitorial timekeeping should let staff submit manual time for missed entries and adjust inaccurate clock-ins, while routing those changes through approval before they affect payroll. It should also check for conflicts such as overlapping entries, so corrections can be resolved before they become part of the final timesheet.

Can I identify late arrivals, short shifts, and absences across cleaning crews?

If the time tracking tool you choose for your cleaning crew supports attendance schedules and exception tagging, you can identify such irregular shifts. It should compare recorded clock-in and clock-out times against expected shift hours, then flag issues such as late arrivals, short hours, early leave, or absence directly on the attendance record.

Can multiple staff members clock in from the same work site or device?

TimeBee’s kiosk mode lets multiple cleaners use the same on-site device while keeping each clock-in tied to the individual employee through a personal PIN. When a cleaner clocks in, the kiosk also captures their face together with the time and location, creating a clearer record of who started work and where.
