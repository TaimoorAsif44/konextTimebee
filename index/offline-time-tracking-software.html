FAQs

Which employee metrics are still tracked without an internet connection?

TimeBee continues tracking essential employee metrics even without an internet connection. While offline, it records activity levels such as mouse clicks and keystrokes, monitors web and app usage, logs time on projects and tasks, captures screenshots, detects idle time, and maintains accurate work timelines. All data is securely stored locally during offline periods and automatically synced once the internet connection is restored. This ensures complete visibility and accurate records without interruptions.

Will offline tracking still record time accurately if the system is shut down or restarted?

Yes, TimeBee ensures accurate time tracking without internet, even if the system is shut down or restarted. This means no time is lost or misrecorded due to connectivity issues or device restarts. With TimeBee’s reliable offline capabilities, you can trust that employee hours are tracked precisely and securely at all times.

Will offline tracking still capture breaks and idle time?

TimeBee’s offline time logger app detects breaks and idle time by monitoring the absence of mouse movement and keystrokes. If no activity is detected for a set period, a pop-up reminder appears on the screen. This feature works fully offline, ensuring accurate tracking of idle periods without internet access. It then graphically represents all this activity in the detailed timelines on the user dashboard.

Is there a limit to how much offline tracking data can be stored locally?

Yes, offline tracking data is stored locally and may be subject to limits based on your device’s storage and the app’s design. Some tools allow extended offline storage for longer periods without internet, while others limit data retention to optimize performance. Always review the tool’s specifications to ensure it meets your offline tracking needs.

Is there a way to manually adjust offline-tracked time if errors are found?

Yes, TimeBee’s offline time tracker app allows employees to submit manual time requests if any work hours are missed during offline periods. Employers can review and approve these requests to correct errors or add missing hours. This feature provides flexibility to handle exceptions and maintain precise data even when tracking occurs without an internet connection.

What happens if an employee deletes or uninstalls the tracking software while offline?

If an employee deletes or uninstalls the offline time tracking app, any unsynced data stored locally will be lost. Since offline records remain on the device until synced, removing the app prematurely can create gaps in time logs. To prevent this, many tools add safeguards like requiring admin permissions, blocking uninstallation during work hours, or notifying supervisors if the app is removed.
