FAQs

Can TimeBee track remote and in-office call center agents?

Yes, our call center employee time tracking software supports remote, in-office, and hybrid models through desktop, mobile, browser, and kiosk workflows. Because administrators can control both organization-wide and user-level settings, tracking remains consistent across different work setups. Even when connectivity is interrupted, TimeBee continues recording offline and syncs the data once the connection is restored.

Can call center hours tracking software manage agents across different shifts and schedules?

TimeBee lets organizations define working days, required hours, shift start and end times, as well as acceptable late-arrival and early-leave margins. When specific users follow different schedules, these organization-wide settings can be adjusted at the user level. Therefore, as teams clock in/out, their attendance records reflect actual hours and relevant exceptions, making it easier for managers to compare recorded work against the schedule.

Does a call center work time tracker record breaks and overtime?

Yes, reliable time tracking software can record breaks, idle periods, total tracked time, and overtime within the same work record. Moreover, when call center employees work beyond their assigned schedules, those extra hours can also be reviewed as overtime and reflected in payroll.

Can a call center time tracking tool monitor agent performance?

Any solution that classifies work activity by productivity can help managers assess performance beyond hours worked. By separating productive, unproductive, neutral, and idle time, it becomes easier to see how effectively agents use their working hours. When that data is supported by activity records such as screenshots, managers gain additional context, too.

Can call center agent time tracking software use tracked hours for payroll?

Once regular hours and overtime are recorded, the customer support time tracker automatically calculates payroll based on the applicable hourly rate. If any corrections, bonuses, or other adjustments are needed, managers can enter an adjustment amount before finalizing the calculation. As a result, the exported payroll files are ready for further payroll processing.

Is BPO time tracking software suitable for small teams and outsourcing companies?

TimeBee supports both small teams and growing outsourcing operations. It lets smaller teams keep oversight simple through clear team roles, while allowing larger setups to organize agents across multiple teams and client-specific projects as operations expand. Plus, since recorded hours remain tied to the relevant project and task, outsourcing companies can scale workforce management without losing visibility or billing accuracy.

Can call center employees clock in/out from different devices?

Call center employees can clock in/out through whichever approved workflow fits their setup, whether desktop, mobile, browser, web extension, or kiosk, so teams do not have to standardize every agent on the same device. That flexibility makes cross-platform BPO time tracking software practical for mixed-device operations.

Does time tracking software for call centers integrate with other tools?

TimeBee integrates with tools commonly used across call center operations, project management, and workplace workflows, including HubSpot, Outlook, Asana, Jira, Trello, ClickUp, etc. Because these integrations connect time tracking with systems that teams already rely on, call centers can manage time and activity without disrupting established workflows.
